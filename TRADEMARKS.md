## Publication safeguards

- The public project name, app name, icon, repository, and package metadata use **NAS Watchdog**, not a third-party manufacturer's brand.
- The app icon and listing graphics must be original NAS Watchdog artwork. Do not use Zettlab/ZettOS logos, product photographs, website screenshots, marketing imagery, fonts, or trade dress in app or store assets.
- Compatibility references use official spellings and are limited to factual, necessary statements about supported community hardware. Do not imply that a compatibility expectation is a manufacturer certification.
- Store descriptions do not use "official", "partner", "approved", "certified", or similar claims without documented authorization.
- Hardware behavior is independently observed through ordinary documented Linux interfaces. No ZettOS firmware, proprietary application, API credential, decompiled source, or redistributed manufacturer asset is bundled in this project.
- Screenshots and support reports must contain original dashboard/app content with redacted personal data—not copyrighted promotional material copied from a manufacturer.
- The official <https://zettlab.com/> website and <https://wiki.zettlab.com/> are linked as external references; MIT licensing here applies only to NAS Watchdog's own project code.

See the vendor's current [terms](https://zettlab.com/pages/terms-of-service), Google's [Impersonation policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9888374), and [Intellectual Property policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9888072) before publishing.
